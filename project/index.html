Sweet Creation by Liss is brought to life by a passionate young baker from Nueva Guinea, Nicaragua. With a love for creating beautiful and delicious cakes, she pours her heart and skill into every order.

From the aroma of fresh-baked treats to the artistry of custom decorations, her work is all about sharing joy through flavor and design. Each cake is not just a dessert—it’s a dream crafted for your special moments.
